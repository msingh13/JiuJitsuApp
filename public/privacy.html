Your journal and privacy

Updated November 11, 2026

Browser journal

Without signing in, journal entries, photos and recordings are stored in this browser. Other people using the same browser profile may see them. Clearing site data can remove them. Download a backup before clearing data or changing devices.

Google sign-in and cloud storage

When enabled, Google sign-in supplies basic account identity, such as your name, email and profile picture. Matcraft does not request access to Google Drive, Gmail or contacts. Supabase stores your signed-in journal and uploaded files. Access rules prevent other app users from accessing your records; the project owner and service providers can administer the service. This is not end-to-end encrypted storage.

Existing browser entries are copied to your account only when you explicitly choose that action. Sign out on shared devices. Free services have storage and availability limits.

Food-photo estimates

This optional feature works only when connected. Selecting “Estimate from this photo” sends that food image and the portion description to Google Gemini. No journal history or Google identity is included in the estimate request. Google's unpaid service may use inputs and outputs to improve its products, including human review. Do not submit sensitive, confidential or personal information. Use food-only images without faces or documents and check your description. This feature is intended for adults 18 and older.

Estimates can be inaccurate and are not medical advice. Review and edit the values before saving. Google Gemini terms and data use.

Exports and deletion

You can export journal records from Settings and download attachments. Complete browser backups include media. Deleting an entry removes the entry, but previously uploaded cloud attachments may remain stored. To request deletion of your cloud account and retained files, contact Matcraft support using the email you signed in with. Do not include private health details in public GitHub issues.

Hosting and services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google provides authentication and, when enabled, food-photo estimates. Supabase provides account and cloud storage services. Those providers process service requests under their own policies. Matcraft does not include advertising trackers in its app code.
